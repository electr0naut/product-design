## Two follow-ups (non-blocking; fix in the next kit change)

1. **The attempt counter is only cleared after a successful completion.** A review that finished but
   *blocked* (on findings or on `advance_main`) leaves the counter at 1. If a human unblocks that same card for
   a second look, the runner refuses it as "already attempted". Clear the counter whenever a review
   *finishes*, blocked or not. Keep it only for failures (transient errors, timeouts and interruptions).
2. **The budget guard turns a card with many findings into a failure.** With a $3 cap and $0.75 per call,
   about four Claude calls fit. If refuting and proving need more, the review errors out and then gives up on
   the next pass. It should degrade instead:
   - when the next call would exceed the cap, stop calling Claude;
   - record the remaining findings as `notes` with `"reason": "budget"`;
   - finish the review normally, and report the skipped count in the summary.
